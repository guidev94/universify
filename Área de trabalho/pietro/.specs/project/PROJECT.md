# M. PIETRO Branding & Gifts

Premium e-commerce platform for corporate gifts and personalized items.

## Vision
To provide a high-end, seamless catalog experience for corporate clients, focusing on branding authority, design excellence, and operational reliability through integration with Stricker Europe API.

## Goals
- Premium "Luxe" UI/UX (Awwwards/Stripe level).
- Real-time product catalog with +1200 items.
- High-performance filtering and search.
- Seamless "Request for Quote" flow via WhatsApp.
- Reliable data synchronization and caching.
